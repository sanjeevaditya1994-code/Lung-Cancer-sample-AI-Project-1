Patient is a 64-year-old male with a 40-pack-year smoking history.

CT chest revealed a 4.2 cm mass in the right upper lobe.

Biopsy confirmed non-small cell lung cancer,
adenocarcinoma type.

PET scan showed involvement of mediastinal lymph nodes.

No distant metastases were identified.

The patient was referred to medical oncology
for treatment planning.
